The problem

Cloud AI raises avoidable risk when your files are the liability.

When sensitive documents are indexed by third-party services, control and auditability can drop. In regulated workflows, that can increase compliance exposure and remediation cost.

When third-party services process case files, privilege, access, and disclosure risk must be actively managed.

Architecture

Privacy is not a setting.
It is how the system is built.

There is no toggle to turn off cloud sync. There is no cloud layer. StratoSort analyzes content, maintains a local search layer, and manages file actions inside one desktop workflow.

Local-first architecture

After model download, core processing stays on-device. In standard configuration, StratoSort does not send document content to third-party services.

On-device AI analysis

Document understanding runs inside the app on your hardware, without depending on third-party AI APIs for core workflows.

Local search and organization

Your search index and organization logic stay on-device, so your files and retrieval layer remain under your control.
